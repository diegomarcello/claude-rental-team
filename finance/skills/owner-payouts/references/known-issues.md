# Known Issues

Found during integration. This file overrides the reference files, which were copied unchanged.

- `europe.md`: the numeric example is the United States one (US dollars or US units), not a European one. Use the formulas only; take currency, units and local rules from the Europe jurisdiction files.

## Delivery 02 (Brazil)

- `brazil.md` declares `plugin: rt-property-management`. This skill is `rt-finance:owner-payouts`.
- `brazil.md` cites Lei 8.245/91, art. 22, VI and VII, as a duty to account to the owner. Inciso VI is the itemized receipt for amounts paid and VII is the payment of administration and brokerage fees. Do not cite them for accounting to the owner.
- `brazil.md` and the calculation spec rely on RIR/2018 art. 687 for deductions. That article was not confirmed. Secondary sources point to art. 688 (withholding) and art. 689 (exclusions). An accountant must confirm in Decreto 9.580/2018.
