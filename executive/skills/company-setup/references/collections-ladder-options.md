# Collections ladder: proposals to choose from

None of these is company policy, and they disagree with each other. Show them to the user and ask them to pick one, adapt one or describe their own. Never choose, recommend or preselect one for them.

| Proposal | Source | Stages (days from due date) |
|---|---|---|
| A | Brazil collections reference | D+1 to D+5 friendly contact with an updated bill; D+6 to D+15 active collection with a warning about credit bureau listing or the guarantee; D+16 to D+30 formal extrajudicial notice and notice to the guarantor; after D+30 end of the friendly phase and eviction file |
| B | Brazil receivables calculation spec | 1 to 15 friendly administrative collection; 16 to 30 extrajudicial notice or protest; over 30 referral to legal |
| C | Example in a company context file | D-5 preventive reminder; D+1 friendly notice; D+5 formal notice; D+15 legal team or credit bureau listing; D+30 eviction filing |

## Ask for each stage

1. First and last day.
2. Action and channel.
3. Who runs it and who approves it.
4. Fees, interest, discounts and instalment plans allowed, and their limits.
5. Deadline to notify the guarantor or insurer, taken from the policy or contract.

The proposals were written for Brazilian leases. For another country, ask for the company's ladder and check it against that country's reference.

The aging bands printed by the receivables script are fixed reporting bands. Changing the ladder does not change them.

## Record the answer

Write it in `company-context.md` under a `## Collections ladder` heading, replacing any earlier example ladder. A lawyer must review notices and legal steps before use. Mark `collections-ladder` as `confirmed` in `Setup status` with the name or role of who decided and the date.
