# Guia de Preenchimento da Seção `Process` (EUA e Europa — 16 arquivos de jurisdição)

Nos 16 arquivos de jurisdição (`united-states` e `europe`), a seção `## Process` estava vazia e deve seguir o padrão operacional abaixo adaptado a cada domínio:

```markdown
## Process
1. **Intake & Scope Verification:** Confirm property address, state/member-state jurisdiction, transaction type, and party roles before applying statutory rules.
2. **Threshold & Exemption Check:** Verify monetary thresholds, entity exemptions, and statutory cooling-off or notice periods applicable to the specific state/country.
3. **Mandatory Disclosures & Documentation:** Collect and validate required statutory forms, ID/KYC records, and written consents prior to execution.
4. **Escalation & Hold Points:** Flag high-risk triggers (e.g., pending litigation, suspended federal rules such as FinCEN RRE or TCPA one-to-one consent, or cross-border tax withholding) for licensed local counsel/CPA review.
5. **Audit Trail & Retention:** Archive executed documents and compliance logs according to statutory retention schedules.
```
